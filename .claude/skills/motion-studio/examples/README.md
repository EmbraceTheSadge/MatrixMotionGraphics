# The three example videos

These are the real prompts the three videos were made from, with machine-specific paths replaced by this
skill's commands. They show what a director's brief looks like at each level. Claude expands a one-line
request into this kind of brief by itself (SKILL.md §1), so you don't need to paste them, but you can.

| prompt | result |
|---|---|
| `prompts/level-1-showreel.txt` (~1.4k chars) | 16 s, 60 fps, pastel showreel, one violet shape that never cuts, 7 labelled techniques, no music |
| `prompts/level-2-lyric-video.txt` (~5.6k chars) | "Sixty Frames Out": 35.6 s, original 140 BPM hyperpop song (take 131 of 12), 9 worlds, every word on its sung onset |
| `prompts/level-3-story-film.txt` (~7.5k chars) | "One Conversation": 68.5 s, 12 shots, a 120 BPM score with the drop at 50.031 s, 375 synthesized sound effects, every shot 8+/10 |

What changed between them: level 1 is one prompt with a look. Level 2 adds music and sound, made locally and
synced to the word. Level 3 is written like a director: a story, a crew, steps it can't skip, and a critique
loop where it grades its own work until it's good.
